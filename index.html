Three steps from wardrobe to outfit

No typing long lists. Take a photo, and the app does the cutting out and sorting.

  1. Add a photo

    Take a picture of a shirt, a pair of shoes or a bag. StyleSync removes the background and asks what it is.

  2. Dress the doll

    Swipe through tops, bottoms and shoes on the doll until it looks right. Save it as an outfit.

  3. Wear it and plan ahead

    Mark what you wore today and plan the coming days. The app keeps track so you don’t repeat yourself.

Your closet stays on your iPhone

There is no account to make and nothing to sign in to. Your photos and outfits are saved on your phone, not on our servers.

  • No ads and no tracking
  • Location is only used to look up the weather
  • Back up your closet to a file whenever you like

Questions

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 15 or newer.

What happens to my clothes if I get a new phone?

Open Settings in the app and make a backup. Save the file to iCloud Drive or send it to yourself, then restore it on the new phone.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose StyleSync. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next payment.

I paid on another phone. How do I get Premium back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Premium in the app and tap Restore.

Something isn’t working. Who do I contact?

Write to jorgefilp@gmail.com and tell us which iPhone and iOS version you use. We answer in English, Portuguese and Danish.
