Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

StyleSync is an iPhone app made by Jorge Filipe Peixoto Silva Luis (“we”). This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your closet is stored on your iPhone, we don’t have an account system or servers that hold your data, and we don’t track you.

What is stored on your iPhone

Everything you add to the app is saved in a database inside the app on your iPhone:

  • photos of your clothes, and the details you give them (type, colour, price, notes)
  • your outfits, your plans and which days you wore what
  • your settings, such as language, theme and reminder time

Photos you take in the app are also saved to an album called “StyleSync” in your Photos library. We never receive any of this. If you delete the app, the data inside it is deleted with it. The album in Photos stays until you delete it.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera and your photo library so you can add clothes. Photos are processed on your iPhone, including removing the background. They are not uploaded.

Location and weather

If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location to show the weather and suggest outfits for it. To get the forecast, the coordinates are sent to the weather service Open-Meteo. Apple’s location service is used to show the name of your town. We don’t store your location, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings on your iPhone.

Notifications

The morning outfit reminder is a notification scheduled on your iPhone. No notification is sent from a server.

Shop check

When you choose to look an item up online, the app opens a Google Shopping search in your browser with the item’s type, colour and name. From that point, Google’s privacy policy applies.

Backups

You can make a backup of your closet as a file. You decide where it goes, for example iCloud Drive, Files or an email to yourself. We never see it.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are bought and handled by Apple through the App Store. We don’t receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app reads Apple’s receipt on your iPhone to check whether Premium is active.

What we don’t do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking
  • No selling or sharing of your data

Children

StyleSync is not made for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you are in full control of it: you can change or delete anything in the app, or delete the app. If you live in the EU you have rights under the GDPR. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to hand over or delete, but you are welcome to ask.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
